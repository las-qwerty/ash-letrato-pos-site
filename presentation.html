ASH LETRATO POS

A complete point-of-sale system for a digital printing business — register, inventory, production, staff, and payroll. Runs fully offline on the devices you already own.

15 modules 3 platforms — Android · Windows · iOS 0 internet required 4 staff roles

The register

Point of Sale

  • Sell in two taps — touch catalog organized by category, with instant search and camera barcode scanning.

  • Area pricing built in — tarpaulins, vinyl and boards bill by the square foot; sizes enter at the line.

  • Every tender — cash, GCash, Maya, card; discounts, loyalty redemption, and notary fees applied automatically.

  • Made for shared counters — staff sign in with a 4-digit PIN and the till auto-locks when idle.

At a glance

Dashboard

  • Live numbers — today's sales, transactions, and a rolling 7-day chart that updates as sales happen.

  • Top products — see what actually moves, ranked by revenue.

  • Low-stock alerts — materials about to run out surface before they stop a job.

  • Branch-aware — staff see their branch; the owner flips between locations or sees all.

Every peso, accounted

Transactions

  • Complete ledger — every sale searchable by invoice, customer, or cashier.

  • Instant receipts — reprint or reshow any receipt, any time.

  • Controlled exceptions — voids and refunds need a supervisor PIN and record who approved.

  • Status clarity — completed, voided, and refunded are visually distinct.

Sellable goods

Products

  • Merch with real stock — mugs, shirts, totes track units left on the shelf.

  • Barcodes & photos — every item can carry a code and an image for faster picking.

  • Materials linked — products can consume inventory automatically at sale time.

Billed work

Services

  • Priced like a print shop thinks — per square foot, per page, per set — not forced into unit pricing.

  • Consumption recipes — a 4×6 tarp pulls vinyl and ink from stock the moment it sells.

  • Machine-tracked work — punching and bind-prep jobs flow into Production for counting.

Where the work happens

Production

  • Job tickets — every machine run gets a scannable ticket tied to the sale that paid for it.

  • Counter reconciliation — opening and closing machine readings are compared to what was actually produced.

  • Waste & reprints — damaged and reprocessed counts are recorded, not lost.

  • Corrections are supervised — fixing a count needs a documented reason and a supervisor PIN.

Materials under control

Inventory

  • Per-branch stock — vinyl at Caloocan is not vinyl at Greenhills; each branch counts its own.

  • Transfers — moving stock between branches is a logged, reversible operation.

  • Stock counts — physical counts write corrections automatically with a movement record.

  • Movement ledger — every sheet in and out is a row: who, what, why, where.

Repeat business

Customers

  • Loyalty that works — points earn on every peso and redeem straight at the till.

  • Legal discounts automated — senior citizen and PWD pricing applies correctly every time.

  • Tiering — silver, gold, platinum levels tracked on lifetime spend.

  • History attached — lifetime spend and purchase patterns on every profile.

Marketing that applies itself

Promotions

  • Three kinds — percent-off, peso-off vouchers, and buy-X-get-Y bundles.

  • Guardrails — date windows, minimum spend, and usage limits built in.

  • Code entry at the till — cashiers apply a code; the math is never manual.

Decisions, on evidence

Reports

  • Any period — today, 7 days, 30 days, or a custom range — with the previous period compared.

  • Branch breakdown — see which location earns and which needs attention.

  • Payment mix — cash vs e-wallet trends for reconciliation.

  • Export to Excel — the whole report becomes a workbook for accounting.

  • Audit trail — every sensitive action is logged with who and when.

Access by design

Staff & Roles

  • Four roles — owner, manager, cashier, handler — each sees only what they need.

  • Branch-bound logins — a Caloocan cashier lives in Caloocan's books, nowhere else.

  • 4-digit PINs, throttled — failed attempts lock the account with escalating timeouts.

  • Delegation without risk — only flagged accounts can approve voids and refunds.

People, paid right

HR & Payroll

  • Attendance built in — clock in/out at the till; leave days are recorded and approved.

  • Payroll from real hours — monthly, daily, or hourly rates plus overtime produce payslips automatically.

  • Approval workflow — draft → review → approve → paid; every step is audited.

  • No second system — the same staff list that runs the register runs payroll.

Grows with you

Branches

  • Multi-location by default — every branch gets its own stock, sales ledger, and staff.

  • One owner view — compare locations side by side or zoom into a single till.

  • Add a branch in seconds — new locations join the system without redeploying anything.

Your business rules

Settings

  • Receipt branding — name, TIN, address, and footer on every printed ticket.

  • Tax & loyalty tuning — VAT rate, senior/PWD percentage, earn and redeem rates.

  • Data portability — one-click export and restore moves the whole business between devices.

  • Auto-lock — the till protects itself after idle minutes you choose.
